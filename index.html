FAQs

Which locations do you serve?

We serve Makati and Ayala Alabang, Muntinlupa.

What types of carpets can you clean?

Share your carpet type and condition in the quote form and we will confirm whether and how we can clean it.

How long does carpet cleaning take?

It depends on the area, carpet type and condition. We give an estimate with your quote.

How should I prepare before the service?

Clear small items and fragile objects from the carpet. We will confirm anything else when we schedule.

Can you treat stains and odors?

We can assess stains and odors and recommend a treatment. Results depend on the stain and the carpet, so we do not promise complete removal.

How much does carpet cleaning cost?

Pricing depends on your carpet and space. Request a personalized quote and we will reply with a clear figure.

How can I schedule an appointment?

Send a quote request, then agree a date and time with us once you receive your quotation.
